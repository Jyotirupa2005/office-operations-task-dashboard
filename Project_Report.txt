OFFICE OPERATIONS & TASK MANAGEMENT DASHBOARD

Project type: Portfolio / simulated business case
Period covered: January-August 2026
Task records: 1,200
Meeting records: 260

PROJECT OVERVIEW
This project analyzes day-to-day office activities in a software-development environment.
It tracks client communication, meeting coordination, manual user testing, screencast creation,
office requirements, management support, documentation and follow-ups.

BUSINESS PROBLEM
Administrative work can be spread across emails, meetings and separate follow-up lists.
A manager needs a quick view of workload, pending work, delays and recurring blockers.
The dashboard brings those activities into one reporting view.

TOOLS
Excel, Python (Pandas/NumPy), MySQL and Power BI.

KEY KPIs
Total Tasks: 1,200
Completed Tasks: 746
Pending Tasks: 157
In Progress: 267
Cancelled: 30
On-Time Completion Rate: 85.39%
Average Delay: 0.35 days

POWER BI PAGES
1. Management Overview – KPI cards, monthly volume, task status and department performance.
2. Task & Workload – task type, priority, assignee workload and pending work.
3. Communication & Meetings – stakeholder activity, channels and meeting attendance.
4. Issues & Delays – blockers, late tasks and delay patterns.

INTERVIEW EXPLANATION
“I created this project as a practical office-operations reporting case. I used a simulated
dataset covering routine activities such as client communication, meeting coordination,
manual user testing, screencast creation, management support and office requirements.
I cleaned and analyzed the data with Python, wrote SQL queries for management questions,
and designed a Power BI dashboard around completion rate, pending work, on-time performance
and delays. The goal was to make it easy for a manager to see what needs follow-up and
where operational issues are occurring.”

DATA NOTE
All names, clients and figures are fictional/simulated for portfolio use. They are designed
to resemble normal office activity but do not represent actual BinaryFolks records.
